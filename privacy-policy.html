Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 8 October 2026

Aarogyam Residents Welfare Association ("ARWA", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Aarogyam One mobile application and associated community management services.

Aarogyam One is designed to support community management, communication, resident services, visitor management and governance for the Aarogyam residential community.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, Aarogyam One may collect and process the following information:

  • Resident information such as name, mobile number, flat number and owner/member information.
  • Visitor information, including visitor name, visit-related information and visitor photographs where required for visitor management.
  • Complaint and service-related information submitted by residents.
  • Membership application information, including information voluntarily provided for membership processing.
  • Aadhaar information where voluntarily provided as part of a membership application.
  • Club booking and community activity information.
  • Society notices, useful service information and other community-related information.
  • Account and authentication information required to provide authorized access.

2. Camera and Photographs

Aarogyam One may request camera access for visitor photographs. Visitor photographs are used for visitor identification and community security purposes.

Camera access is not intended for continuous monitoring or recording of the user's surroundings.

3. Notifications

Aarogyam One may use device notifications to provide relevant community alerts, visitor-related notifications, service updates, notices or other important information.

4. Internet and Online Services

Aarogyam One requires internet connectivity for features that communicate with online services, including authentication, community records, document or image storage and synchronization.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through Aarogyam One may be used to:

  • Provide resident and community management services.
  • Manage visitors and visitor approvals.
  • Receive and process complaints.
  • Process membership applications.
  • Manage club bookings and community facilities.
  • Publish society notices and governance documents.
  • Provide useful community services and directories.
  • Maintain appropriate community security and administration.
  • Communicate important information to authorized users.
  • Maintain records required for legitimate administrative or legal purposes.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be accessible to authorized association personnel and authorized service providers where reasonably necessary to operate Aarogyam One, provide requested services, maintain the platform, or comply with applicable law.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, government authority or to protect the rights, safety or security of the community and its users.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain community records, administer the association, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations or protect legitimate interests.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements, users may contact the association to request access to, correction of, or appropriate deletion of their personal information.

10. Children’s Privacy

Aarogyam One is intended for community residents and authorized users. It is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children for independent use of the service.

11. Medical and Emergency Information

Aarogyam One may provide directories or contact information for medical and emergency services. Such information is provided as community reference information.

Aarogyam One does not diagnose medical conditions, provide medical treatment, provide medical advice, or itself provide emergency medical care.

12. Third-Party Technology Services

Aarogyam One may use third-party technology and infrastructure services for functions such as cloud database services, authentication, storage, hosting and technical operation of the platform.

Such services may process information as necessary to provide their respective technical functions and are subject to their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Aarogyam One platform, applicable requirements or community operations.

The updated version will be published through the applicable Aarogyam One web or application service.

14. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, requests or concerns regarding Aarogyam One, please contact Aarogyam Residents Welfare Association using the official contact email associated with the service.
