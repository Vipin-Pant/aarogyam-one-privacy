Aarogyam One — Account and Data Deletion

How to request account deletion

Aarogyam One is a residential society management application operated for Aarogyam residents and the Aarogyam Residents Welfare Association (ARWA).

Residents who wish to request deletion of their Aarogyam One account may email:

Email: vpant2001@gmail.com

Subject: Aarogyam One — Account Deletion Request

Please include your registered mobile number and flat number so the team can verify your account. Do not email your password or OTP.

What happens after a request?

After verifying the request, the Aarogyam One team will review and process it. Personal information that is no longer required will be deleted or de-identified where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Certain society-related records, including complaints, visitor records, membership records, and financial or administrative records, may need to be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate association record-keeping obligations.

Where retention is necessary, records will be retained only for the applicable period and handled in accordance with the privacy policy. Data in backups may remain until the applicable backup-retention cycle expires.
